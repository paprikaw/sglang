## 1 Similar Attention efficiency; different Expert efficiency

Conceptual two-layer deployment, read top to bottom. EP2 partitions the E routed experts of each layer equally across both GPUs and dispatches/combines tokens at every layer. PP2 places layer L1 on stage 1/GPU 0 and layer L2 on stage 2/GPU 1, keeping all E experts of each local layer. Stage activations cross the PP boundary. The PP Expert block is twice the width of an EP E/2 block: width encodes expert count, not latency. The recorded protocol uses Attention TP=1, global batch 8, local Attention batch 4, EP Attention DP=2, and two PP microbatch slots. Matching local Attention shapes supports similar expected per-call efficiency at matched contexts; total batch alone does not guarantee this for arbitrary microbatching. More local experts and fewer tokens per expert can reduce PP Expert reuse. Compare Expert execution against dispatch/combine, while retaining PP stage communication. This is a placement diagram; the next three slides contain measurements.

## 2 GPU platform can reverse the EP / PP winner

E2E is formal mean complete-request latency across three saved rounds. Output throughput is the mean of per-round output tokens / cohort makespan, in tokens/s. Each round has 8 requests producing 128 tokens each (1024 output tokens in total). The cached 4096-token prefix and single fresh input token are unchanged. The lower row replaces the previous TTFT plots with output throughput for A100 and H100. Native platform baselines: A100 Marlin/eager and H100 DeepGEMM/Graph. Expert scope is routed GEMM only. D+C covers DeepEP GPU kernels. Normalize by actual layer calls because EP and PP place different numbers of layers on each card. PP communication is NCCL GPU residency including wait. Profiled activities are independent of formal latency and are not additive E2E slices. Both E2E and throughput favor PP2 on A100 and EP2 on H100.

## 3 PP pays a different Expert cost for each model

E2E and TTFT are formal mean per-request latencies across three rounds. TTFT means time to first streamed token within this workload. Decode starts from 4096 cached tokens, extends by 1 fresh token and produces 128 output tokens; its TTFT does not include a cold 4096-token prefill. Prefill is cold, 4096 fresh tokens and 1 output token. Prefill input throughput is the mean of per-round fresh input tokens / cohort makespan, reported in k tokens/s, and is not derived by dividing tokens by mean request latency. Profiled GPU activities are independent of formal latency and are not additive E2E slices. PP communication is NCCL GPU residency including wait, not wire-only latency. These are the named 35B BF16 and 122B FP8 checkpoints, not a size-only ablation. Right chart shows routed Expert module GPU kernel activity for the complete B8 Prefill cohort, averaged over ranks; shared Expert is excluded. Historical 122B PP Prefill uses ready-send; the 35B baseline does not. The small-model EP Prefill varies across the three saved rounds.
